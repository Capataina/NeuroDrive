# Exercise: Reason About Returns And Advantages

## Goal

Show that you can reason about delayed reward and advantage estimation rather than only repeating definitions.

## Starting Point

Read:

- `concepts/foundations/probability-value-estimation-and-return.md`
- `concepts/core/actor-critic-and-gae.md`

## Tasks

- write a short three-step fictitious driving sequence,
- assign simple rewards and value estimates,
- compute TD error for at least one step,
- explain in plain English whether the action at that step was better or worse than expected.

## Hints

- Use simple numbers.
- The quality of the explanation matters more than algebraic sophistication.
