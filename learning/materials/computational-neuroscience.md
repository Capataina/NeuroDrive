# Materials: Computational Neuroscience

## Why This Topic Matters Here

The README’s central ambition depends on more than loose brain metaphors. To understand the intended future direction, you need some real grounding in:

- Hebbian learning,
- STDP,
- eligibility traces,
- neuromodulation,
- structural plasticity,
- dynamical neural systems.

## What To Look For

Prefer resources that distinguish clearly between:

- established biological findings,
- simplified computational models,
- speculative engineering translations.

That distinction matters because NeuroDrive is an engineering project drawing inspiration from neuroscience, not a pure biology simulator.

1. Hebbian learning and local plasticity

4. neuromodulation and reinforcement signals
